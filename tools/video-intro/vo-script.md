# Mexican Airstrips intro video — voice-over script

Source: the "Mexican Airstrips — Intro Video Script" Claude Doc (16-Nov-2026). One take per line. Run
`python3 tools/video-intro/update-recorder.py` after editing to copy these lines into site/record/index.html.
Airstrip codes (XSQA, XSBY, RCX) are shown as text over, never spoken.

### `s1-a.m4a` — 1 · Cold open (0:08)

Same airstrip, San Pedro de la Cueva, Sonora. One of these is real.

### `s1-b.m4a` — 1 · Title (0:15)

I'm Wayne... our free GeezerGeek Pilot app puts Mexico's remote airstrips, Baja and mainland, into ForeFlight and X-Plane.

### `s1-c.m4a` — 1 · The problem (0:25)

On this trip, all ForeFlight had was a waypoint I'd typed in by latitude and longitude. No runway, no ramp, no pattern.

### `s1-d.m4a` — 1 · Why (0:35)

Airstrips like this get our FlyingDocs volunteers to the free medical and dental clinics we provide. Mexico's FAA counterpart, AFAC, publishes the official spreadsheet of more than 1,500 airstrips, about 500 of them currently permitted.

### `s2-a.m4a` — 2 · Find the airstrip (0:50)

Find the airstrip by name or code, and you get AFAC's record: 4,265 feet of asphalt, runway 16/34, day only. English by default, Spanish with a tap.

### `s2-b.m4a` — 2 · Fix the runway (1:05)

AFAC's coordinates are sometimes off. At Laguna San Ignacio, the whale-watching spot, just drag the runway end to where the runway surface starts.

### `s2-c.m4a` — 2 · Preview (1:20)

The preview shows the runway by surface, the ramp, and a dashed traffic pattern, whose width is adjustable.

### `s2-d.m4a` — 2 · Into ForeFlight (1:32–1:45, one take)

On your iPad or iPhone, tap Open in ForeFlight, install the pack under More, Custom Content, and turn on the two new layers: the drawing, and a named point mid-runway. There it is.

### `s2-e.m4a` — 2 · Flight plan (2:00)

Tap on an airport waypoint, or type its name, to add it to your flight plan.

### `s2-f.m4a` — 2 · Before and after (2:15)

Beats hand-typed coordinates.

### `s3-a.m4a` — 3 · X-Plane (2:25)

Now let's detail an airstrip for X-Plane. Here's Scorpion Bay, on Baja's Pacific side.

### `s3-b.m4a` — 3 · Ramp (2:40)

No mapped ramp here, so the app adds one. Drag it and size it to the imagery.

### `s3-c.m4a` — 3 · Tie-downs and objects (2:52)

Tie-downs are where you'll start, so put them on the ramp, not in the cactus. Then a windsock, a hangar, a fuel tank.

### `s3-d.m4a` — 3 · Saved layout (3:12)

Your layout's saved, so you can come back and keep fiddling.

### `s3-e.m4a` — 3 · Install (3:20)

Download the zip, unzip it into Custom Scenery, done.

### `s3-f.m4a` — 3 · Gateway (3:30)

If X-Plane already has an airport there, Import from Gateway lets you build on it.

### `s3-g.m4a` — 3 · Parked (3:40, after the sim landing)

And parked right where I put the tie-down.

### `s4-a.m4a` — 4 · Caution (4:25)

One caution: this app is for planning and preparation for your flight, in real life or on the sim. It doesn't tell you whether an airstrip is open, in good shape, or needs permission. Check before you go.
